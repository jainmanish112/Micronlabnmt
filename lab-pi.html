Lab PI

Dr. Manish Jain

Principal Investigator

manish.jain@nmt.edu

Assistant Professor

Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering

Dr. Manish Jain is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering at New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology. His research focuses on hierarchical materials design, structure-property correlations under extreme conditions, thin film synthesis, and machine learning-assisted high-throughput materials discovery. He has extensive experience in nano/microscale mechanics and in-situ SEM mechanical testing, with postdoctoral positions at Sandia National Laboratories, University of New South Wales, and the Swiss Federal Laboratory for Materials Science and Technology.
